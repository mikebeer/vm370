# R-01: every geometry shift site, enumerated

> **Status, 4 October 2026.** The shifts were converted as part of the DAT sweep (XA0033–XA0037DK, [29-DAT-CONVERSION.md](29-DAT-CONVERSION.md)) and CP runs on the result: IPL from DASD, CMS to `Ready;`, `DEF STOR 64M` ([30-STATE.md](30-STATE.md)). The named-symbol mitigation (`PAGSHFT`, `SEGSHFT`, `PTLSHFT`, `KEYSHFT`) was not adopted as written; the authority became `DMKVAT`'s own `CODEB0` row (`PAGSHFT H'10'`, `SEGSHFT H'18'`, masks `X'000FF000'`/`X'7FF00000'`), checked mechanically by `codeb0.py` and `geomchk.py` after `I-188` showed a wrong segment mask costing a build cycle. Two corrections to the table: `SRL ,11` sites are a halfword-PTE stride and become 10, not `KEYSHFT` 12 (`I-190`); and the list was taken from the base tree, so it includes cards the APAR chain removes and misses sites it adds (`I-176`, `applied.py`). Sites this table never had became walls 25 and 29 (`DMKPTR SEGEXA`, `DMKPGS CKSEG`; `I-196`, `I-206` in [13-ISSUES.md](13-ISSUES.md)). `R-01` is retired for the tables; the `SLL R1,4+4` note stands and was load-bearing.

Generated by `../arch/31bit/tools/shifts.py`, re-runnable against a moving tree:

    python3 arch/31bit/tools/shifts.py /path/to/vmce/source/cp --table

**70 sites must change**, across nine modules. Each is a bare number encoding
the 64 KB-segment, 16-pages-per-segment or 2 KB-key geometry. No symbol rename
finds them and a missed one does not fail — it returns a plausible real address
for the wrong page, which is why this is the only weight-9 risk in the register.

The mitigation is to give each a **name before changing any geometry**, so the
change has one definition point:

| Symbol | Now | Becomes | Encodes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PAGSHFT` | 4 | **8** | pages per segment: 16 → 256 |
| `SEGSHFT` | 16 | **20** | segment size: 64 KB → 1 MB |
| `PTLSHFT` | 6 | **10** | bytes per page table: ×64 → ×1024 |
| `KEYSHFT` | 11 | **12** | storage-key block: 2 KB → 4 KB *(superseded — see status note; these are halfword-PTE strides and become 10, `I-190`)* |

`12` stays 12 — pages remain 4 KB under ESA/390, so every page-offset
extraction survives untouched. 146 shift sites in these modules are in that
category and need no attention at all.

## The one compound site

`DMKBLD` line 232 is `SLL R1,4+4`, commented `SEGMENT COUNT * 16`, and it is the
reason this table exists rather than a `sed` script:

    IC    R1,VMSEG       SEGMENT COUNT/16 -1
    LA    R1,1(,R1)      SEGMENT COUNT/16
    SLL   R1,4+4         SEGMENT COUNT * 16

`(segcount/16) × 256 = segcount × 16`, so the comment is correct and **the two
4s are different facts**: one undoes `VMSEG`'s own `/16` packing, the other is
the pages-per-segment conversion. Only the second becomes 8. The site is
`4+PAGSHFT` — not `PAGSHFT+4`, not `8+4`. Arithmetically identical; naming the
wrong term would record the wrong fact for whoever reads it next.

Incidentally that settles a related question: `VMSEG`'s one-byte field holds
`(segment count / 16) - 1`, capping segments at 16 × 256 = **4096**. ESA/390
with 1 MB segments needs 2048, so unlike `DMKBLDRT`'s packed halfword
(`09-DAT-WORK-SHAPE.md` §1) this field survives unchanged.
